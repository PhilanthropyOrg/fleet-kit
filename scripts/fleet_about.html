Reading the dashboard

The Home page is split into four rows. Here is what each one tells you.

Outcome
The goal above, and the steps that lead to it. Is the product getting the results it exists for?
Output
What the fleet finished: problems solved, changes put live, and requests from the owner that got done.
Flow
What is waiting or stuck: the to-do list, open changes, items that need a clearer description, and messages between workers.
Health and spend
Is it alive? How many AI accounts are working, and what it costs.
Issue
One item on the to-do list: a bug, a missing feature, or a finding.
Backlog
The whole to-do list, ranked.
Pull request (PR)
A proposed code change, waiting to be checked.
Merge
The change was approved and joined the main code.
Live (deployed)
The change is now on the real site people use.
Run
One turn of one worker. Click any run on Home to see what it did.

What people do

The fleet works without anyone watching. People steer it and step in when it matters.

The owner
  • Sets the goal and the direction the work is ranked against.
  • Asks for things. Those requests jump the queue ("Your requests" on Home).
  • Gets three short emails a day: what landed, and anything that needs a person.
Anyone signed in
  • Pause the whole fleet with the Fleet switch at the top right of Home.
  • Switch one worker on or off from the Roster page.
  • Ask the fleet a question, or ask it to do something, in the chat.
  • Every action is logged under your name.
Built-in safety
  • No change goes live without an AI review and the automated tests passing.
  • If the site or a test breaks, a fixer opens a fix or rolls the change back within the hour.
  • Each worker has a time and spending limit per run, and every run is recorded, even ones that did nothing.

Have a question? Open the dashboard and click the chat button in the bottom corner. Ask in plain words, like "what is stuck?" or "pause nerd".
